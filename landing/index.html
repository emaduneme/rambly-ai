Your words, not AI's

Rambly keeps your ideas and the way you say them. It takes out the ums, smooths the flow and puts things in order. You choose how much it edits.

Your recordings

We'll publish Rambly's privacy policy for recordings before anyone gets access. The waitlist only stores your email and the answers you choose to give.

Nothing to set up

Just talk the way you'd explain your work to a friend. No outline, no special commands, no training the software first.

Why talking works

Researchers have studied dictating first drafts, known as the mind-to-paper method, for over a decade.

You outline, then say the draft out loud. In one study, even junior researchers produced good first drafts this way. Read the study

How much does it cost?

What happens to my recordings?

We'll publish Rambly's privacy policy for recordings before anyone gets access. For now, the waitlist only stores your email and the answers you choose to give. Waitlist privacy

Is this an AI writing tool?

Not in the "write my paper" sense. Rambly works only with what you say. It cleans up and organizes your words; the ideas and the argument stay yours.

What can I use it on?

Rambly runs in your web browser, on your phone or your computer.
