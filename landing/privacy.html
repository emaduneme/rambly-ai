Waitlist privacy

This page covers the Rambly waitlist. Rambly is run by Emmanuel Maduneme.

What we collect

Your email address, and if you answer them, your role and what you'd mainly use Rambly for. We also record how you found the page (for example, a Reddit post or a flyer) and the date you signed up.

Why

To invite you when Rambly is ready, to send occasional updates, and to understand who wants Rambly and where they heard about it.

Where it's stored

The list is kept in Kit, the email service we use to send waitlist emails. We keep your information until you unsubscribe. We never sell it.

Visitor counts

We count visits with Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that doesn't use cookies or track you across sites. It tells us how many people came from each link we share.

Unsubscribing

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Click it and you're off the list. You can also email us and we'll remove you.

Recordings

The waitlist doesn't collect any recordings. We'll publish Rambly's privacy policy for recordings before anyone gets access.
